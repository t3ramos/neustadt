Neustadt includes Three.js, three-mesh-bvh, three-gpu-pathtracer, cannon-es, Lucide,
DM Sans and Manrope. Their license texts are included in this directory.
The pathtracer also includes the glslSmartDeNoise shader by Michele Morrone
under the BSD 2-Clause license, reproduced in glslSmartDeNoise.txt.
Own game source, procedural models and the generated Neustadt cover are
independent original assets for this project.
